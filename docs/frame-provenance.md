`apps/frame` was imported on 2026-10-17 from the owner-supplied
`Mosaicast-Frame-Integration-Handoff-2026-10-17.zip`, source repository
[Desperado/home-entertainment](https://github.com/Desperado/home-entertainment).
The archive snapshot is based on `b0cb105b2a8eed527813f7f749809dffccb43a01`
plus its included pending TV patch. Before import, the original workspace was
compared read-only at `d699796947a56c56ccccd36a67690e1b8eccf8d7`.
Newer README, `app/app.js`, `app/index.html` and `app/themes.css` were carried
forward before integration. The original workspace was not edited.

No top-level license was present in that source snapshot. In this implementation
session the owner explicitly authorized importing the source into this MIT
repository, stating they own or have rights to reuse it, with provenance and
font/asset notices preserved. This authorization covers imported project code;
third-party rights remain governed by their own terms.

The bundled fonts retain all seven SIL OFL notices and the original source table
in [fonts/README.md](../apps/frame/app/fonts/README.md). The official Yamaha logo
retains its [asset notice](../apps/frame/app/assets/README.md); the MIT license does
not relicense Yamaha's logo or trademarks. `artist-orbits.svg` and the artist-scene
presentation are carried from the source project. Album artwork and LRCLIB lyrics
are fetched content, not repository-licensed assets. Existing source README hardware
reports are historical evidence, not validation of the new companion integration.

Generated bundles, personal device presets, dependencies, signing assets and
credentials were excluded from import. TV source remains in its own workspace
package; its remote navigation, local themes and metadata behavior are preserved.
