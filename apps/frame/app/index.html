    Playback & sources
    Audio plays on your Yamaha. The Play key can play a focused collection when supported.
    Browse opens a menu without changing playback. Switch input selects a source with network/USB autoplay disabled; it sends no power command. Streaming services may require an existing account and may no longer be available in your region.
    Sources shows the inputs and capabilities reported by your Yamaha. Pairing, network setup, CD controls and multiroom group setup are managed with MusicCast or the Yamaha remote. Radio controls in companion mode use the capabilities reported by your Yamaha.
    
        
    Connection & power
    Standby stops listening. Remote power on requires Network Standby enabled in MusicCast or the Yamaha settings.
